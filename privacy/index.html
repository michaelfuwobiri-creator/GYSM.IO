GYSM Stream Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

GYSM Stream is a film app for Amazon Fire TV. We built it to work without collecting personal information. This page explains what the app stores, what it sends over the internet, and the choices you have.

What we collect

Nothing. GYSM Stream has no accounts, no sign-up, no advertising and no analytics. We do not collect your name, email address, location, device identifiers or viewing history, and we do not sell or share any information about you.

What the app stores on your device

To work, the app keeps the following only on your Fire TV. It is never sent to us:

  • your settings (for example the storage limit and download quality)
  • the titles you add to My List
  • where you stopped in each film, so you can resume
  • films, artwork and subtitles you download, or that smart downloads saves for you
  • a total of how much data offline viewing has saved

You can remove downloads at any time on the Downloads screen. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Settings → Applications on your Fire TV, deletes all of it.

Internet connections

To show and play films, the app connects only to:

  • GYSM’s own film storage on GitHub (github.com and its download servers), which holds every film, its artwork and the list of available films.

Like any website or app, these servers receive standard technical information that comes with every internet request, such as your IP address and the file requested. We use our server logs only to deliver films and keep the service running, and we do not combine them with any other information.

The app also checks whether your network is metered, so it can avoid downloading on mobile data if you have chosen that. This check happens on your device and is not sent anywhere.

Children

GYSM Stream is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children. Some films are rated 16+; you can hide them in Settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
